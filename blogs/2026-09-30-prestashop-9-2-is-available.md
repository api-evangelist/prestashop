PrestaShop 9.2 is here, and it is a big one! 🎉 This release brings some of the most requested features of recent years straight into the platform: a native One Page Checkout module to make buying faster and smoother, an AI assistant right inside the back office with Ask AI, and a brand new Extra Properties system that lets developers extend any core entity natively, and merchants create their own custom fields directly from the back office. That’s not all; this release also ships another batch of pages migrated to Symfony, and a long list of improvements and bug fixes.
